A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Straight answers for your next upload.

Can every photo fit under 100 KB?

Not at every resolution and quality. Detailed images need more bytes. Enable smaller dimensions to make another tradeoff, or raise the target. We clearly label outputs that remain over your target.

Will a 600 × 600 preset make an exact square?

It selects a centered crop to 600 × 600. If enlargement is disabled and the original is too small, the tool asks you to allow enlargement or reduce the dimensions. Fit-inside mode instead preserves the full image.

Are transparency and animation preserved?

PNG and WebP can retain transparency; JPEG is flattened onto white. Animated GIF, WebP or AVIF files are decoded as a single still frame. Export does not preserve animation or original metadata.

Why is PNG sometimes bigger?

PNG is lossless and does not use the quality setting. A photograph may encode much smaller as JPEG or WebP. A small target for PNG may require reducing dimensions.
